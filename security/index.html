    Security

    Your clients' data, treated as if it were ours.

    Outworx is built for regulated workflows. Here's how we keep your firm and your clients safe.

    Breach notification

    If a breach affects your data, we tell you within 48 hours so you can meet your own ICO deadline.

    Encryption everywhere

    TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest. Customer data is encrypted with per-tenant keys.

    UK hosting

    All production data stored in AWS eu-west-2 (London). Any sub-processor outside the UK is covered by UK transfer safeguards.

    UK & EU GDPR

    Registered with the ICO (C2043012). Data Processing Agreement published at /dpa, with the full sub-processor list.

    SSO & MFA

    SAML SSO available on Business plans. TOTP and WebAuthn supported for all users.

    Least-privilege access

    Production access is role-based, logged, and reviewed quarterly. No customer data on engineer laptops.

    Request our security pack

    Security questionnaire answers and a signed copy of our Data Processing Agreement are available on request.
