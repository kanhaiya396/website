    Why We Built Outworx

    Accounting firms didn't need more data.
    They needed less manual work.

    The accounting industry already had document storage, bookkeeping software and disconnected automation tools.

    What was missing was a system that could capture documents, validate information, prepare transactions and help firms maintain ledger quality inside a single workflow.

    That's why Outworx exists.

    Our Process

    How Outworx works

    Five clear stages — from inbox to posted transaction — with humans firmly in the loop.

    1. Capture

      Documents arrive from email, WhatsApp, client portals and drag-and-drop — all landing in one unified queue.

    2. Categorise

      Line items are classified against your chart of accounts using historical posting context, not brittle per-supplier rules.

    3. Validate

      Totals, dates and supplier details are cross-checked, and duplicates are flagged before anything posts.

    4. Review

      An exception-first queue surfaces only what needs human eyes — with inline editing and full source attachments.

    5. Post

      One click pushes approved batches to Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or Nomi — with the source document attached.
